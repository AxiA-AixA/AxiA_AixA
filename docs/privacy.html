Privacy notice

Last updated 19 November 2026

Who we are

AxiA is a tool for designers that is being built by a small team in London. For anything about your data, email qoon@boogpunt.com.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist we keep your name (if you give it), your email address, the time you signed up and the page you signed up from. We don’t collect anything else, and this site doesn’t use cookies for tracking or advertising.

Why we use it

To email you about AxiA early access and, now and then, about what we’re building. We do this because you chose to join the waitlist (your consent), and you can change your mind at any time.

Where it’s kept

  • Sign-ups are stored in a Google Sheet, and we’re notified by email through Google. Google processes this data for us and may store it outside the UK under its standard safeguards.
  • This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps short technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security.
  • The page loads its typeface from Adobe Fonts.

How long we keep it

Until early access opens and for up to 12 months after that, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.

Who we share it with

No one. We don’t sell your details or pass them to anyone else for marketing.

Your rights

You can ask to see the details we hold about you, correct them, delete them, or stop hearing from us. Just email qoon@boogpunt.com and we’ll do it. If you’re not happy with how we’ve handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
